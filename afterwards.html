Afterwards Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Afterwards mobile application (“Afterwards”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use the app.

1. Information you provide

Afterwards lets you describe life events and manage plans and tasks. Information you enter may include event descriptions, task text, dates, reminder settings and completion status.

2. AI-assisted suggestions

When you request AI-assisted suggestions, the event or task text needed to fulfil that request is sent from the app to the Afterwards backend and an AI service provider. This processing is used to generate practical task suggestions for you. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising.

3. Local storage

Saved plans, tasks, progress and related preferences are stored locally on your device. Afterwards does not currently require an account or sign-in and does not provide cloud synchronisation for saved plans.

4. Notifications and reminders

If you enable reminders, Afterwards may request Android notification permission. This permission is used to display reminders on your device.

5. Information we do not currently use

Afterwards does not currently use advertising, analytics or tracking services. It does not request access to contacts, photos, precise location, financial information or health information unless a future version clearly introduces such a feature and this policy is updated.

6. Retention and deletion

Local plans and tasks remain on your device until you edit or delete them, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Text sent for AI suggestions may be processed temporarily by our backend and AI service provider to provide the requested feature, maintain security and comply with law. For questions about deletion of information processed through the service, contact us using the address below.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled through the Afterwards backend. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children’s privacy

Afterwards is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features, data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact
